THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE BAKES

Our Purrfect Story

Purrfectly Bakes was inspired by us, the fur moms behind the business! I'm a proud dog lover, while my business partner is a cat lover. Our love for our furry companions inspired us to create the name "Purrfectly Bakes," reflecting our personalities and passion for baking.

The business originally started as a college entrepreneurship project. We began with just one product: banana loaf. At first, it was only a side hustle while we were studying and exploring our passion for baking.

Last year, we decided to take a big risk and turn our small side business into a full-time venture. Thankfully, Purrfectly Bakes is still running today! We're grateful for the journey, the support, and every customer who has been part of our story. We hope to continue growing and sharing our baked goods with more people! 🐶🐱💗
